# Launch PRD — the platform, launched by itself

Who: solo founders who can build but cannot afford dishonest distribution,
and engineers who want a harness whose gates they can try to break. What
pain: every claim-shaped tool asks for trust before evidence; this launch
leads with an offline audit trail that needs no key. Why now: the loop
closed (v0.13 through v0.21), the benchmark page publishes its own failing
row, and the evidence discipline is only useful if someone else runs it.

The kill criterion is aimed at ourselves, verbatim from the design: if the
product-bench build rate falls below 60% or the probe pass rate below 50%
for two consecutive weekly runs, scope is cut at Gate PL5. (A second
criterion on weekly maintenance hours stood here until v0.81.0 and was
withdrawn — ADR-033 — because its only instrument was a number someone had
to type each week.) The registry's first entry may well be
one of our own launch hypotheses — that would be the strongest trust
artifact this launch could produce.
